# Verify CV

Check the CV for factual and structural risk.

Look for:
- invented or unsupported metrics
- vague impact claims
- missing links
- empty bullets
- duplicated bullets
- date/title/company inconsistencies
- claims that need human confirmation

Use labels:
- `[Unverified]` for claims needing confirmation
- `[Missing]` for absent source data
- `[Suggestion]` for improvements the human may provide
